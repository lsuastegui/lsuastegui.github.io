I'm Luis, a malware analyst and reverse engineer based in Ontario, Canada.

I take malware apart to understand how it works and how to catch it. Some posts follow a sample from unpacking to detection; others focus on a single technique or something new I ran into along the way.

## What you'll find here

- Malware analysis: loaders, infostealers, RATs and whatever else turns out to be interesting
- Techniques worth a closer look: evasion, obfuscation, persistence and anything new I come across
- Command-and-control traffic decoded, with the network signatures that match it
- YARA and Sigma rules for what I analyze
- Configuration extractors and small analysis tools in Python
- Notes on tooling, including AI-assisted reverse engineering

## Background

I've spent more than six years in incident response, threat research, and detection engineering in Canada and Latin America, working with managed security services, a CSIRT, and the financial sector. I've also built hands-on security training labs, from reverse engineering and incident response to offensive scenarios, and spoken at security conferences.

## A note on samples

Everything analyzed here comes from public sources. Nothing on this blog is taken from client or employer incidents, and the views are my own.
